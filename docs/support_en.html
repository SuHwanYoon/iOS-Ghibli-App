❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Do I need an account to use the app?
A. No. Dotori Library requires no login, account creation, or personal information. You can explore all film details freely.
Q. Can I view my favorite films offline?
A. Yes. Your favorite films and recently fetched data are cached securely on your device, allowing you to access them even when offline.
Q. How do I change the theme or language?
A. Go to the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar to switch between Light/Dark mode and change your preferred language (Korean/English/Japanese).
