Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026
Summary: 'Dotori Library' does not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data or device identifiers.

1. Personal Information We Collect

The 'Dotori Library' application does not require user registration or login. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Local Device Storage (UserDefaults)

To provide a smooth user experience, the app stores only standard preference information locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults API:

  • Appearance theme preference (System Default / Light / Dark)
  • App language preference (System / Korean / English / Japanese)
  • List of film identifiers marked as favorites

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server. Deleting the app permanently removes this local data from your device.

3. Third-Party APIs and Network Communication

The app fetches publicly accessible film and character information from an open REST API (Ghibli REST API). During these network requests, no user identifiers or personal data are transmitted.

4. Intellectual Property & Disclaimer

'Dotori Library' is an unofficial, non-commercial fan-made guide application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Studio Ghibli. All movie information, images, and character assets belong to their respective copyright owners.
